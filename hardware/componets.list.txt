COMPONENTS LIST – WAKEWISE PROJECT

1. Vibration Sensor (SW-420)
   Detects sudden movement or prolonged inactivity.

2. Motion Sensor (MPU6050)
   Detects head tilt and nodding behavior.

3. Microcontroller (Arduino Nano / ESP32)
   Processes sensor data and controls alert logic.

4. Vibration Motor
   Generates vibration alert to wake the driver.

5. Rechargeable Battery (3.7V Li-ion)
   Supplies power to the system.

6. Smart Sunglasses Frame
   Houses all components in a wearable form.
